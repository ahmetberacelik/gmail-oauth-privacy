Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-18

Who operates this integration

This is a private, single-user Gmail integration operated by Ahmet Bera Çelik for personal productivity. It is not offered to the public and does not provide accounts for other people.

What data is accessed

When the owner requests an operation, the integration may access the Gmail data needed for that operation, such as message content and headers, threads, labels, drafts, and attachments. It also uses OAuth credentials required to maintain the connection to Google.

How data is used

Data is used only to perform the owner-requested Gmail operation: searching, reading, organizing, drafting, replying to, or sending email. Sending an email is performed only after the owner explicitly requests it.

Storage and sharing

OAuth credentials are stored on the owner’s computer. The integration does not sell Gmail data or use it for advertising. When the owner asks an assistant to summarize or transform email content, the requested content may be processed by the AI provider configured by the owner; it is not shared as a separate commercial dataset.

Retention and deletion

The owner can revoke access at any time from the Google Account security settings and can delete the local OAuth credentials and local application data. Gmail messages and drafts remain subject to Google’s own account controls and retention policies.

Security

The integration uses Google OAuth and requests only the Gmail permissions needed for its operations. No password, payment information, or verification code is collected by this site.

Contact

For questions about this policy, visit the operator’s GitHub profile.
